The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to inform the users of how data is collected, stored and the rights the users have regarding their data. 

1. Collection of the personally identifiable information

This application collects a minimal amount of personally identifiable information (PII) necessary for system functionality. The data collected includes usernames, IP addresses, timestamps and user behavior through actions sent to the robot.

2. Basis for processing the data

The data is collected to ensure system accountability of user actions, robot safety monitoring, and compliance with audit trail functionalities. Logging user commands enables traceability to prevent misuse that may lead to malfunction or security incidents.

3. How the data is secured

All processed data is securely stored and accessed using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Only users with "Auditor" role privileges may access mission logs. Data is only retained for as long as necessary to support audit and safety functionalities, after which it will be permanently removed in accordance with GDPR principles of storage limitations. The data is not shared with third-parties. Users may request to delete their personal data, subject to crucial audit and and safety retention requirements.
